Auditorium & Events

Visvesvaraya Central Auditorium during Tech Fest 2026

Central Block, Level 2, GPCET

State-of-the-art air-conditioned auditorium accommodating over 1,200 delegates. Equipped with Dolby surround sound, concert acoustics, and massive 4K LED backdrops hosting the IGNITOR National Fest and ETECIS International Conference.

Innovation & Labs

AICTE IDEA Lab & Computer Engineering Center

Innovatia Makers Forge, GPCET

AICTE-sanctioned Idea Development, Evaluation & Application (IDEA) Lab providing students hands-on engineering exposure with industrial 3D printers, laser cutters, PCB milling machines, and high-performance computing clusters.
